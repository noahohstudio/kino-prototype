# Kino, a design prototype

Kino is Beli for movies, with a friend network: rank films through quick head-to-heads, see how your taste matches your friends', and pick films together as a group.

This is the coded prototype of design V.2, with its first motion pass. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is saved; the people are invented. On a phone you get the iPhone layout; at 900px and wider, the web layout.

**Open it:** https://noahohstudio.github.io/kino-prototype/

Film posters belong to their studios and appear here for a non-commercial design study.
