# Kino, a design prototype

Kino is Beli for movies, with a friend network: rank films through quick head-to-heads, see how your taste matches your friends', and pick films together as a group.

This is the coded prototype of design V.2.1, passed. On top of the bell, a feed that doesn't end (with trailers) and the web's left rail, it now has piles on Explore that emerge from the stack under a frosted header, a Rank what you watched row that grows with what you've seen, new notifications and your turn marked on the avatar, more room from the screen edges on iPhone, and a roomier rank sheet with bigger posters. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is saved; the people are invented. On a phone you get the iPhone layout; at 900px and wider, the web layout.

**Open it:** https://noahohstudio.github.io/kino-prototype/ (the web with a top bar instead of the rail: https://noahohstudio.github.io/kino-prototype/?nav=top)
